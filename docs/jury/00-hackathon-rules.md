## Our challenge: 02 — Powering the Future of Work

- Build an enterprise-focused AI solution with multi-step agentic or
  rule-based workflows, realistic future-of-work use cases, production-style
  web app running on Vultr.
- Explicit use cases include HR & Recruitment: screen candidates, schedule
  interviews, coordinate onboarding, track employee workflows.
- Platform thinking, not a one-off local demo. Serverless Inference optional.
- Challenge 2 teams may use deployment helpers (Supabase/Coolify on Vultr).
- REVERSA fit: enterprise customer/employee onboarding across systems with
  verified execution and self-healing — squarely in scope.

## Submission (required on lablab.ai for judging)

- Public demo URL, recorded demo video, cover image, slide presentation,
  demo application platform.
- Containment moment in video is required for Challenge 1 only.
- Vultr Community showcase is optional extra exposure, not a substitute.
- Must be original and MIT-compliant.

## Judging criteria

1. Application of Technology. 2. Presentation (clarity/effectiveness).
3. Business Value. 4. Originality.

  2nd: $3,000 + $1,000 credits. 3rd: $1,000. Distribution up to 90 days.
