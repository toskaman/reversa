# 01 — Project overview

**REVERSA — Self-Healing AI Operations Platform.**
AI that doesn't just act. It makes things right.

Enterprise teams run multi-system operations (onboarding, support, RevOps)
through brittle automations. When step 6 of 10 fails, nobody undoes steps
1–5. REVERSA is the control layer that plans the operation, executes it
across systems, verifies every effect, and heals recoverable failures
itself — with proof.

**One-line demo:** onboard Acme Corporation across 5 systems in 10 verified
steps; inject a billing outage mid-run; watch detection, reverse-order
compensation, verified restoration, and the incident report.
